# Bibliography

Citation keys are shared with code comments and tests. These references support
the research plan; they do not establish completion of this repository's census.

## Voronoi complexes and K-theory

- **[DSEKM19]** M. Dutour Sikirić, P. Elbaz-Vincent, A. Kupers, J. Martinet.
  *Voronoi complexes in higher dimensions, cohomology of $GL_N(\mathbb Z)$ for
  $N\geq8$ and the triviality of $K_8(\mathbb Z)$.*
  [arXiv:1910.11598](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.11598),
  [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/S1474748025101394).
  Main source for Watson enumeration, realizability, reference counts, and
  orientation conventions (§§2–4); §6 treats the K-theory application.
- **[EVGS13]** P. Elbaz-Vincent, H. Gangl, C. Soulé.
  *Perfect forms, K-theory and the cohomology of modular groups.*
  Adv. Math. 245 (2013), 587–624.
  [arXiv:1001.0789](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.0789),
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2013.06.014).
  Earlier computations and conventions in smaller lattice ranks.
- **[EVGS02]** P. Elbaz-Vincent, H. Gangl, C. Soulé.
  *Quelques calculs de la cohomologie de $GL_N(\mathbb Z)$ et de la K-théorie de
  $\mathbb Z$.* C. R. Math. Acad. Sci. Paris 335 (2002), 321–324.
  [arXiv:math/0207067](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0207067).
- **[Kup19]** A. Kupers. *A short proof that $K_8(\mathbb Z)\cong0$.*
  Note dated October 21, 2019.
  [Author's PDF](https://www.utsc.utoronto.ca/people/kupers/wp-content/uploads/sites/50/2021/01/k8zshorter.pdf).
  Related proof strategy; any degree-12 extension needs its own hypotheses.

## Enumeration and exact computation

- **[DHS13]** M. Dutour Sikirić, K. Hulek, A. Schürmann.
  *Smoothness and singularities of the perfect form and the second Voronoi
  compactification of $\mathcal A_g$.*
  [arXiv:1303.5846](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.5846).
  Reference for simplicial ranges of perfect cones.
- **[KMS12]** W. Keller, J. Martinet, A. Schürmann, with an appendix by
  M. Dutour Sikirić. *On classifying Minkowskian sublattices.*
  Math. Comp. 81 (2012), 1063–1092.
  [arXiv:0904.3110](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.3110).
- **[PS97]** W. Plesken, B. Souvignier. *Computing isometries of lattices.*
  J. Symbolic Comput. 24 (1997), 327–334.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1006/jsco.1996.0130).
- **[DEGU07]** J.-G. Dumas, P. Elbaz-Vincent, P. Giorgi, A. Urbańska.
  *Parallel computation of the rank of large sparse matrices from algebraic
  K-theory.* PASCO '07 (2007), 43–52.
  [arXiv:0704.2351](https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.2351),
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/1278177.1278186).
- **[DSV01]** J.-G. Dumas, B. D. Saunders, G. Villard.
  *On efficient sparse integer matrix Smith normal form computations.*
  J. Symbolic Comput. 32 (2001), 71–99.
